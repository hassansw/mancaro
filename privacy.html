Mancaro Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Mancaro does not collect any personal data.

Mancaro is an offline trade simulation game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress, achievements and settings (theme, sound, vibration, currency) on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No data is sent to any server, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Permissions

The game asks Android for permission to vibrate, used only for short haptic feedback when you tap. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Backups

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the game's save file in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Google under your account settings, not by Mancaro.

Deleting your data

Use Restart career in the game menu to reset your progress, or uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings to delete everything.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
